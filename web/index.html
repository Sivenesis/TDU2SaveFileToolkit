Online vs. Offline Profile Architecture & Progression Transfer
TDU2 stores online profile status in byte 256 of ProfileList.dat (0xFF = Online, 0x00 = Offline) and IsOnlineEnabledProfile in the encrypted OPTIONS container.
Use this panel to switch profiles between Online and Offline, configure credentials, or clone progression from an offline save directly into an authentic, server-registered online profile.

Fixes "Invalid Nickname" Server Rejection

TDU2 multiplayer and community servers validate registered nicknames. If an offline profile cannot connect because its nickname is not registered on the multiplayer server, transfer its full progression (cars, cash, houses, licenses, road discovery) into an already-registered online profile.

The target profile's online server nickname, 8-byte Profile UUID, DLC tokens (Extends), and login credentials are 100% preserved.

Controls & Steering Wheel Bindings (KEYMAP) are strictly protected and will remain untouched.
